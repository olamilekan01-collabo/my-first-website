Phone Battery Guide

How to Make Your Phone Battery Last Longer

Does your phone battery run low before the end of the day? A few changes to your settings and daily habits may help you get more time from each charge.

1. Reduce screen brightness

A bright screen can consume considerable battery power. Lower the brightness or enable adaptive brightness if your phone supports it.

2. Turn on Battery Saver

Battery Saver can reduce background activity and other power use. Find it in your phone's Battery settings. The exact menu varies by manufacturer.

3. Check which apps use the most power

Open Settings and look for Battery or Battery Usage. Review the list to identify apps using more power than expected. Restrict background activity when appropriate.

4. Manage location and connectivity

Review which apps have permission to access your location. Remove unnecessary permissions. In areas with weak mobile coverage, your phone may use extra power searching for a signal.

5. Keep your phone at a comfortable temperature

Excessive heat can damage a battery over time. Avoid leaving your phone in direct sunlight or a hot car, and use a suitable charger recommended for your device.

6. Keep your software updated

Install updates from your phone's official settings when available. Updates can include fixes for battery and performance problems.

Frequently asked questions

Should I always close every app?

No. You generally do not need to force-close every app. Focus on apps with unusually high battery use.

Does dark mode save battery?

It can save power on many phones with OLED screens. The benefit depends on the screen technology and settings.

Conclusion

Start by adjusting brightness, checking battery usage and using Battery Saver when needed. If your battery suddenly drains much faster than usual, check for recent app changes or seek help from a qualified technician.
